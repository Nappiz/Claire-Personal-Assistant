# Repository Instructions

## Mandatory refactor policy

Before performing any refactor in this repository, read and follow
[`REFACTORING_RULES.md`](REFACTORING_RULES.md) in full. For backend refactors,
also read the applicable stage in
[`backend/Analysis/enterprise_backend_refactor_plan.md`](backend/Analysis/enterprise_backend_refactor_plan.md).

These documents are mandatory for human contributors and AI agents. They do not
authorize a refactor by themselves; the task scope and user authorization still
control what may be changed.
